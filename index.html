Hermes

Hermes is a private, single-user assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It uses two Google OAuth apps:

  • Hermes TroyDesktop: reads the owner's Gmail, Calendar and Contacts, reads and completes the owner's Google Tasks, and creates and updates files in one Google Drive folder that it made itself. It cannot send, delete or share email or files.
  • Hermes Skills Backup: stores backups of the assistant's own configuration in Google Drive files that it made itself.

See the privacy policy.
